# Senior Data Scientist — India Product/Retail Company Preparation

## Purpose

This document captures the strategy discussed for preparing for **Senior Data Scientist opportunities in India**, with a particular focus on **retail, fashion, home, e-commerce, and consumer-product companies**.

The immediate benchmark company is **Walmart Global Tech India (Bangalore)**, but the goal is **not to prepare only for Walmart**. The preparation should build a reusable Senior Data Scientist profile that can be applied across multiple product companies.

---

# 1. Target Career Positioning

## Recommended positioning

> **Senior Retail Data Scientist — Forecasting, Pricing, Optimization & Decision Science**

The key advantage is the combination of:

- 8+ years of Data Science experience
- 5+ years of retail Data Science experience
- Demand forecasting
- Markdown optimization
- Price elasticity
- Inventory/returns optimization
- Machine learning
- Production-oriented analytics
- Experience with real retail business problems

The narrative should **not** be:

> "I am trying to break into a product company."

A stronger narrative is:

> "I am an experienced retail data scientist who has worked on real production retail problems and now want to take that experience into a product-led retail organization."

This positioning makes the preparation more relevant to Walmart, Target, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, H&M, Amazon and similar organizations.

# 6. Is Walmart a Secure Job?

Separate two concepts:

### Company stability

Walmart is a very large global retailer, and Global Tech is a core technology organization.

### Individual job security

No private-sector Data Science role should be considered guaranteed.

Employee-review data is mixed. Some employees report good WLB and compensation, while others mention reorganizations, leadership changes and workload variability.

### Practical conclusion

Think of Walmart as:

- **Large/stable company:** yes
- **Core technology organization:** yes
- **Reasonable job stability:** generally
- **Guaranteed job security:** no
- **Consistent experience across all teams:** no

---

# 7. Questions to Ask During the Interview

Because team-level variation matters, ask questions such as:

1. **How long has this team existed?**
2. **What are the team's priorities for the next 12 months?**
3. **How is Data Science involved in product/business decisions?**
4. **How much of the role is modeling versus analytics?**
5. **What does production deployment look like?**
6. **How large is the Data Science/ML team?**
7. **What happened to the team during recent organizational changes?**
8. **How is success measured for this role?**
9. **What is the expected scope for a Senior Data Scientist?**
10. **What opportunities exist to move toward Staff/Lead responsibilities?**

These questions help evaluate the specific role rather than relying only on the Walmart brand.

# 20. Interview Story Structure

For each project, prepare a consistent structure:

### 1. Business problem

What problem were we solving?

### 2. Why it mattered

What was the business impact?

### 3. Data

What data was available?

### 4. Baseline

What simple approach did we start with?

### 5. Modeling

Why did we select the model?

### 6. Validation

How did we test it?

### 7. Challenges

What went wrong?

### 8. Trade-offs

Why did we choose one approach over another?

### 9. Deployment

How did the model reach production?

### 10. Monitoring

How did we detect degradation?

### 11. Business impact

What changed because of the solution?

### 12. What you would improve

This last part is particularly useful for Senior-level interviews.

# 22. What NOT to Over-Focus On

Avoid spending disproportionate time on:

- Hard competitive programming
- Extremely obscure algorithms
- Memorizing every ML algorithm
- Learning every new GenAI framework
- Building many small portfolio projects
- Generic LeetCode grinding without DS relevance

The goal is not:

> "Become an algorithm expert."

The goal is:

> **Become a strong Senior Data Scientist candidate who can solve business problems using statistics, ML, SQL, Python and sound engineering/product judgment.**
